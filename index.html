High Arctic Expedition

30 Jun – 25 Sep 202676 days sailingEllesmere Island

Iceland to Ellesmere Island by way of the west coast of Greenland, then a basecamp at Tanquary Fjord and a ski-mountaineering push onto the glacier to climb Mount Barbeau — and, a hundred kilometres south-west of it, a peak that has never been climbed by anybody.

Ellesmere is the tenth largest island on earth and has a hundred and fifty permanent residents. The channels leading to it are uncharted, shoal-ridden and choked with multi-year ice. Tanquary Fjord lies 720 km from the North Pole.

Circumnavigation of Lake Baikal

From 20 Feb 20261,400 km53°NUnsupported

A full circuit of the frozen shoreline of the oldest and deepest lake on earth, on ski, hauling everything from the start with no resupply. South to north, then back again. Between forty-five and fifty-five days on the ice at −25°C to −40°C.

It has been attempted and never completed. With Mark McInerney.

Trans Greenland Expedition

2024100 daysNine crewCaptain

A hundred days and nine people, combining a sail passage with a man-hauled crossing of the world’s largest ice cap. Sixty-kilo pulks, crevasses, polar bears and a late-season weather window. Stafford captained it.

About

Captain and expedition leader

Stafford Tyrrell is an Irish expedition leader and sailor with more than ten years of expeditions behind him, from the Himalayas to Africa and the Middle East. He has crossed all three of Iceland’s largest ice caps — Vatnajökull, Langjökull and Mýrdalsjökull — and has over seven hundred days in glaciated terrain.

He was part of the first Irish team to climb Mount Damavand in winter, and finished the Marathon des Sables in 2024. He is a commercial offshore diver, a 22-metre captain with fifteen years at sea, and in 2024 he captained a nine-person, hundred-day expedition to Greenland.

He is a Wilderness EMT, ice-field safety trained, a search and rescue member and an AIMG hard ice guide. He is a keen ice climber. He is based in Ireland.

Working with brands

Kit that works in Ireland in February is not kit that works at eighty-two degrees north. Stafford puts equipment into genuinely unforgiving conditions and reports back honestly — publicly if it performs, privately if it doesn’t.

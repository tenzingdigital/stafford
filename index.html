About

A slow apprenticeship in cold places

Stafford Tyrrell is a polar traveller and sailor. He came to it the long way round — crewing deliveries, learning to navigate, then learning what a body does at thirty below when the nearest help is four days away.

The work is unfashionably ordinary: miles under the keel, kilometres under the ski. He is interested in the parts of an expedition nobody photographs. The rationing. The repair in the dark. The decision to turn round, and the decision not to.

He is based in Ireland and travels north whenever the season allows.

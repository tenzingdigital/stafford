Onto the frozen surface of the oldest and deepest lake on earth, on ski, hauling everything from the start with no resupply, at **−25°C to −40°C**. With Mark McInerney.

The objective is the full circumnavigation of the shoreline — south to north and back, 1,400 kilometres. It has been attempted many times and nobody has completed it.
